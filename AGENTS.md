# Agent instructions

> ⚠️ **MANDATORY INSTRUCTION FOR ALL AI AGENTS & MODELS**:
> Every agent and model **MUST read [`TASKS.md`](TASKS.md) before planning or running any tasks**. `TASKS.md` is the authoritative bible and roadmap for all new game features (dynamic weathers, retro shaders, Polish & Tokyo city tracks, campaign mode, and streamlined menus). Always consult and align with `TASKS.md` before writing or modifying code.


## Current game

The active game is the Godot project in [`godot/`](godot/). Open it with
`godot --path godot` or open [`godot/project.godot`](godot/project.godot) in the Godot editor.
Make new gameplay, UI, and rendering changes in this Godot project.

The former browser game at the repository root is **not** a guideline any more (user, 2026-11-10): the
Godot game has long outgrown it in scope. Do not consult it for behaviour, design or feature decisions,
and do not hold new work back to match it. The Godot code and [`TASKS.md`](TASKS.md) are the reference.

Track data is shared/generated from the repository's `tools/` sources. The parity tests under
`godot/tests/` (`run_tests.gd`, `_run_race_test.gd`) stay as regression checks on the physics and race
sim: an unintended change should still keep them passing, but an intended behaviour change may move
them. Say so in the handoff and re-baseline the reference values rather than gating the change off.

## 3D models (Blender)

Blender is installed at `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender` and runs headless. Models are
generated by Python scripts under [`tools/blender/`](tools/blender/), so change the script and rebuild
rather than editing a GLB by hand. The car is
[`tools/blender/build_seicento.py`](tools/blender/build_seicento.py), which writes
`godot/models/seicento.glb`:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b -P tools/blender/build_seicento.py -- --renders /tmp/seicento
```

`--renders DIR` saves preview PNGs (front, rear, side, top and three-quarter views). Look at them before
opening Godot. `--blend FILE` also saves the .blend. `race_car_visual.gd` finds the meshes by object name
(`Body`, `Details`, `Stripes`, `Wheel`) and the per-car surfaces by material name (`Paint`, `Taillight`,
`Glass`, `Headlight`), so keep those names stable.

The trackside structures come from [`tools/blender/build_trackside.py`](tools/blender/build_trackside.py), which writes
`godot/models/trackside.glb`. It builds modular pieces: grandstand bays, tiers, crowd and sponsor boards, pit
garages and race control, the start gantry, and TV towers. `race_world.gd` lays them out along each circuit.
Shared mesh helpers are in [`tools/blender/kit.py`](tools/blender/kit.py). Keep the object names and the layout
constants (`STAND_*`, `PIT_*`, `GANTRY_BEAM_Y` in `race_world.gd`) in step with the script. The seated crowd is
coloured by `shaders/crowd.gdshader` from codes in its vertex colours, so it looks green and orange in Blender.

Other Blender scripts follow the same pattern: `build_trees.py` (`trees.glb`, the species the surroundings plant),
`build_furniture.py` (`furniture.glb`, marshal posts, braking boards, hoardings, bridges, paddock, tyre walls) and
`build_landmarks.py` (`landmarks.glb`: Indianapolis Pagoda and scoring pylon, Suzuka Ferris wheel, Le Mans tyre
bridge, Monza podium, Nürburg castle, Casino de Monte-Carlo, Silverstone Wing, harbour yachts); `build_roadside.py`
(`roadside.glb`, low-poly bushes, grass, hay bales, wayside crosses, kilometre posts and rally boards). The surroundings
(`tools/fetch_surroundings.py` then `tools/build_surroundings.py`) come from OpenStreetMap and are baked to
`godot/data/tracks/<key>_env.json`; add a landmark by naming its OSM element in `LANDMARKS` in
`build_surroundings.py` and its nominal footprint in `LANDMARK_SIZE` in `surroundings.gd`.

### Adding the next car (recipe, from the VW Golf IV pilot)

The cars after the Seicento are catalogue entries (`godot/scripts/data/car_catalog.gd`, `CarCatalog`). Copy the Golf:

1. **Model**: copy [`tools/blender/build_golf4.py`](tools/blender/build_golf4.py) to `build_<car>.py` and change the real-car numbers
   (`LENGTH`, `WHEELBASE`, `FRONT_AXLE_S`, `TRACK_*`, `TYRE_R/W`, `ARCH_R`), the profile curves (`W ZM WSH ZSH ZT ZB WR DR`, nose to
   tail in metres), the glass stations (`S_COWL S_WS_TOP S_SAIL S_B0 S_B1 S_QR S_Q1 S_RG0 S_RG1`, door cuts `S_DOOR`), the lamp outlines
   and the wheel. The generic helpers (loft curves, `MB`, `Projector`, dent shape keys, previews) are in
   [`tools/blender/carkit.py`](tools/blender/carkit.py) (`build_seicento.py` keeps its own copy: its GLB is rebuilt byte-identical).
   Run it with `--renders DIR` and LOOK at every view; lamps are best projected straight (`front_proj`/`rear_proj`, metres across and up),
   a projection that misses the body raises "projection missed"; Blender exits 0 even on a Python error, so read the log.
   Keep the names `race_car_visual.gd` finds: objects `Body Details Stripes Wheel Cabin Martini Abarth Marlboro` (blend shapes `dent_front/
   rear/left/right` on `Body Details Stripes` and the liveries) and materials `Paint Glass Taillight TailBulb TailGlass Headlight LampGlass
   Plate Bumper Trim Gasket Grille` (Paint is the only surface tinted per car and only the Body mesh may carry it, so body-coloured mirrors
   and bumpers go in the Body loft, not in Details). Origin: on the ground midway between the axles, front towards -Z after export.
2. **Physics**: a spec script like [`golf4.gd`](godot/scripts/data/golf4.gd) (a duplicate of `seicento.tres` with `resource_name` set to
   the key: a non-empty name is what makes `Track.profile_for`, `Race` (brake_k) and the strategist treat it as another car) and
   measure it: `godot --headless --path godot -s tests/_car_probe.gd` prints 0-100, 400 m, top speed, braking from 100 km/h and
   skidpad g for the Seicento and the Golf (add the new car to its `cars` dictionary). Tune the spec (final drive, efficiency, `grip_k`,
   mass), never the physics, until the numbers are within a few percent of the real car's. `grip_k` 1.4 is the Seicento's calibration
   (0.96 g); 195/65 R15 road tyres sit near 1.19 (0.86 g).
3. **Catalogue**: add the key to `CarCatalog.KEYS` (append: the order is the saved picker/open-world index), `RaceSettings.car_class`'s
   `@export_enum`, and the `match` arms of `display_name`, `spec` and `visual` (a `*_VISUAL` dictionary: GLB path, wheel radius, wheelbase,
   half tracks, roof-number, brake-light, tailpipe and `fx_axle` points, contact-shadow size, and the `decal_aims` rays that place the door,
   sunstrip, bonnet and sill decals on the Body by raycast: nothing is measured by hand). A car is unlocked when the campaign save's
   `cars` list holds its key (`CarCatalog.unlocked`; the campaign sets it, no shop or fan gate), or with the dev menu's unlock everything.
   The pickers (race set-up CAR, open-world CAR button), `RaceCarVisual.car_key`/`set_car()`, `FreeCar.fit_model` and
   `Game._models_for` need no change. The whole field drives the chosen car.
4. **Tests**: `tests/test_model_contract.gd` (add the GLB to `CONTRACT` and its body/wheel size to `SIZES`), `tests/test_car_catalog.gd`
   (performance band against the real car, the model's size against the spec, wheels at the axles, decals on the body, a headless race
   on two circuits, calibration and profile of its own), then `tests/run_tests.gd` and the parity race `tests/_run_race_test.gd`.
   Look at it in a window afterwards: decals, plate, lamps at night, wheels in the arches, chase-camera distance for a longer car.

## Sounds

The player's engine is synthesised live (`godot/scripts/ui/race_audio.gd`). Every other sound (other cars,
kerbs, gravel, rain, crowd, impacts, pit crews) is baked by [`tools/build_sounds.py`](tools/build_sounds.py)
(numpy) into `godot/audio/*.wav`, so change the script and rebuild (`python3 tools/build_sounds.py --stats`)
rather than editing a WAV, then let Godot reimport (`godot --headless --path godot --import`).
`world_audio.gd` plays them. Its `ENGINE_RPM` and `KERB_RATE` must match the script's.

## Campaign open world (Brzeszcze)

The campaign's free-roam map lives in [`godot/scripts/world/`](godot/scripts/world/) (`open_world.gd` is the mode; game.gd
calls `_enter_open_world`). Its data is baked from OpenStreetMap and EU-DEM by
[`tools/build_open_world.py`](tools/build_open_world.py) into `godot/data/world/brzeszcze*.{json,png}`, so change the
script and rebuild rather than editing the JSON. Its props are `godot/models/town.glb` from
[`tools/blender/build_town.py`](tools/blender/build_town.py). `town_builder.gd` finds them by object name (`Headframe`,
`ChurchSpire`, `Streetlamp`, `TrafficLight`, `BusShelter`, `LevelCrossing`, `FestivalStage`, `FestivalArch`, `Tent`,
`FlagPole`, `CarHatch`, `CarSedan`, `CarVan`, `Maluch`) and the tintable surfaces by material name (`Paint`, `Lamp`,
`Banner`), so keep those names stable. The stage beacons map campaign tracks to the baker's named spots
(`OpenWorld.STAGE_SPOTS`).

The baker also writes a backward-compatible `fences` list to `brzeszcze.json`: mapped OSM barrier ways plus
generated residential plot boundaries with driveway gaps. `TownBuilder.build_fences()` instances the town fence
mesh and roadside shrubs, while `TownMap` hashes the same segments for `FreeCar` collision. Garden and allotment
tree density is baked alongside these features.

The playable area is not a rectangle: `PLAY_POLY` in the baker (metres from the origin) outlines Brzeszcze, Jawiszowice, Wola, Rajsko and
the eastern part of Góra with the S1 corridor. It is baked as `play`; `TownMap.edge_push()` holds the car inside it (1.5 m inset) and
the HUD map dims beyond it. Every road that reaches the edge ends in a barrier (`closures`, built by `TownBuilder.build_closures()`,
collision is a style-4 `fences` segment); the S1's two carriageways are joined by a baked U-turn loop at each end. A tree belt hides
the edge. Content (buildings, trees, lamps) is cut `CONTENT_MARGIN` beyond it; change the polygon, delete the cached OSM and
elevation files if the bounding box grows (elevation reuses nodes already fetched), rebuild `tools/gugik_buildings.py`, then the baker.

Underpasses and bridge solids: the baker finds every street that crosses a railway standing on a bridge or embankment (and every
`tunnel=yes` street crossing a railway), lifts that railway to `UNDERPASS_Z` (5.4 m) and bakes the crossings as `tunnels` in
`brzeszcze.json` (centre, railway and street directions, street width, deck height). `TownBuilder.build_tunnels()` builds the lined bore
(`Tunnels` group: `Portals` mesh, `TunnelLamps`, one `TunnelLight`), `_bank_quad()` cuts the embankment along the street, and `TownMap`
adds side walls and `tunnel_portals()`. The road itself stays at ground height. `TownMap._build_bridge_solids()` makes the bridge piers
(`piers`, `has_pier()` decides which ones TownBuilder draws; only a car below the deck hits them) and the deck parapets (`parapets_near()`;
only a car at the deck's height hits them). `tests/test_open_world_bridges.gd` covers all of it; `tests/_tunnel_shots.gd` is the windowed probe.

The hand-modelled Brzeszcze landmarks in `godot/models/brzeszcze_landmarks.glb` are generated by
[`tools/blender/build_brzeszcze.py`](tools/blender/build_brzeszcze.py); rebuild the script rather than editing the GLB.

Points of interest: [`godot/scripts/world/poi.gd`](godot/scripts/world/poi.gd) (`Poi`) lists the speed traps (`Poi.find_traps`) and the
discoverable landmarks (places within 80 m merged) with their state in `WorldProgress` (unknown / known / discovered), the marker
dictionaries (`kind: "poi"`) the HUD draws on the satnav (nearest 8 inside the disc, no labels) and the big map, and the info lines.
A new kind (drift zones, mission beacons) is an entry in `Poi.KINDS` plus a finder; `tests/test_poi.gd` covers it.

Live events (Forza Horizon style, raced in free roam with no track load): [`godot/scripts/world/live_events.gd`](godot/scripts/world/live_events.gd)
(`LiveEvents`) keeps a board of up to 4 events generated round the car on the map's road graph (`TownMap.route`): street
sprints through checkpoints, speed zones (average speed on a straight main road), drift zones (`DriftScore` on a twisty
stretch) and trailblazers (any way to a far point). Each lasts 5-9 game hours, is replaced when it expires, is cleared or
is left 2.6 km behind, and keeps clear of the stage and mission beacons. Medal targets come from `LiveEvents.pace_time` (a
reference run with the driven car's `PACE` numbers), so faster cars face harder clocks. `OpenWorld` puts them at the end
of `events` with `"live"` set (beacon, card, map marker kind `"live"`, GPS) and runs them in `_update_live` (countdown hold,
checkpoint beacon `live_cp`, HUD `set_live`, ABANDON EVENT on the pause menu); medals go to `WorldProgress.live`.
`tests/test_live_events.gd` covers it; `tests/_live_events_probe.gd` times the generation (about 3 ms each).
The campaign missions (`Missions`) are raced on the map the same way: [`godot/scripts/world/map_missions.gd`](godot/scripts/world/map_missions.gd)
(`MapMissions.event`) takes each mission's route from its baked track file (the routes are in the open world's metres) and
LiveEvents runs it with a field from [`street_rivals.gd`](godot/scripts/world/street_rivals.gd) (`StreetRivals`: cars on the
route polyline with a grip/accel/brake envelope, drag grid four abreast, knockout eliminations). A rally mission is its special
stages in turn with the player driving the liaisons; the crews' times come from `pace_time`. During a mission the traffic is
kept off the roads (`OpenWorld._roads_closed`), the clock jumps to the mission's hour, and the result goes through
`Missions.record` as a raced one did (`OpenWorld._mission_finished`). **Missions happen only on the open-world map**
(user, 2026-11-10): the campaign stage list no longer lists them, `Game._on_world_stage` ignores a mission index, and a
mission whose route is not on the map gets no beacon. The old Bór circuit is not on the map's roads, so the knockout runs one
lap of Mały Bór cut into sectors (`MapMissions.OFF_MAP`, `ev.sectors`) and the rally leaves the Bór stage out. The game's
mission race code (`Missions.apply_settings`, `judge`, the mission rally flow) is no longer reached. The campaign stages
(circuits, most in other towns) still load their own tracks.
