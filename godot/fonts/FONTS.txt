Every skin face covers Polish (ąćęłńóśźż). None of the text faces have ◂ ▸ or Ⓐ Ⓑ Ⓧ Ⓨ, so every font
CmrStyle hands out falls back to the two Noto Symbols fonts, then Godot's default font.
